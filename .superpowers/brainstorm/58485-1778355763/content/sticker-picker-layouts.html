Sticker picker direction

Choose the interaction model for FlyFy sticker packs in chat. The assets are original FlyFy-style packs, grouped like Telegram, and available to every user.

TRIP

A. Telegram-like bottom picker

Recommended. Sticker button opens a bottom panel with recent stickers, pack tabs, grouped grid, search, loading/empty/error states.

B. Full sticker hub

Good for browsing many packs and editorial discovery, but slower inside an active chat and heavier for MVP.

C. Lightweight inline row

Fast to build and compact, but weak for grouped packs, search, and a Telegram-like feeling.
